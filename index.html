漢字Testr Privacy Policy

Effective November 24, 2026

漢字Testr (“the app”) is a kanji and vocabulary study app for iPhone, iPad and Mac. This policy explains what happens to your data when you use it. The short version: the app does not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal data.

Data the app collects

None. The app has no user accounts, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking and no third-party SDKs. It does not connect to any server operated by the developer.

Data stored on your device

Your decks, study progress, review history and settings (such as font choices and review targets) are stored only on your device. The developer cannot see or access this data. Deleting the app deletes it.

Sharing decks

You can choose to share a deck as a file, a link or a code. The deck’s contents are encoded into that file, link or code on your device and sent only where you send it (for example, through Messages or AirDrop). Nothing passes through a server run by the developer. Importing a deck works the same way, in reverse.

Links to other websites

Some screens contain links to outside websites, such as dictionary lookups on jisho.org or the sources credited in Acknowledgements. Those open in your browser and are covered by those sites’ own privacy policies.

Beta testing through TestFlight

If you test a beta version through Apple’s TestFlight, Apple may share certain information with the developer, such as crash reports, device and OS details, the feedback and screenshots you choose to submit, and the name or email address you were invited with. This is handled by Apple under Apple’s Privacy Policy and is used only to fix bugs and improve the app.

Children

Because the app collects no personal data, it collects none from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be raised by opening an issue at github.com/cozmiccle/kanjitestr-privacy/issues.
