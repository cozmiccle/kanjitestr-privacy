Frequently asked questions

Do I need an account or an internet connection?

No. The dictionary is built into the app, and everything works offline. There is no sign-in.

Where is my progress stored?

Only on your device. Your decks, review history and settings are not uploaded anywhere. They are included in your device’s normal backups, but deleting the app deletes them.

How do I share a deck with someone?

Open the deck and choose Share. You can send it as a file, a link or a short code, and you can lock it with a password. The other person opens the file or link, or pastes the code into Import, to add a copy of the deck.

A deck someone sent me says it is locked.

Ask the person who shared it for the password, then enter it when importing.

Can I change the fonts?

Yes. Fonts for kanji and for example sentences can be changed in Settings.

Where does the dictionary data come from?

KANJIDIC2 and JMdict from the Electronic Dictionary Research and Development Group, stroke order from KanjiVG, and example sentences from the Tatoeba project. Full credits are in the app under Acknowledgements.
